# 📦 RetailIQ: Smart Analytics for Grocery E-Commerce Sales

## 📌 Project Overview

The **RetailIQ: Smart Analytics for Grocery E-Commerce Sales** project uncovers actionable insights from sales data across grocery outlets. By utilizing **Power BI** for interactive visual storytelling and robust **data transformation** techniques, the dashboard addresses core business questions around product performance and outlet efficiency.

---

## 📊 Business Objectives

- Discover high-performing products and outlet segments  
- Analyze customer preferences based on sales trends and ratings  
- Evaluate outlet effectiveness based on size, type, and geography  
- Enable strategic decisions for inventory and marketing improvements  

---

## 🔍 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Sales**  
  Measures the overall revenue generated across all products and outlets.

- **Average Sales**  
  Calculates mean sales per transaction or outlet.

- **Number of Items Sold**  
  Represents total product movement across categories and locations.

- **Average Ratings**  
  Evaluates customer satisfaction by item category.

- **Sales by Fat Content and Item Type**  
  Assesses sales patterns across product nutritional profiles.

- **Sales by Outlet Size and Fat Content**  
  Analyzes how outlet capacity influences performance and consumer choices.

- **Sales by Outlet Type**  
  Compares grocery chains, supermarkets, and other outlet formats.

- **Sales by Outlet Location**  
  Breaks down revenue generation by regional footprint.

---

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Interactive reporting and dashboarding  
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and preparation  
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Custom metric calculations  
- **Excel / CSV** – Source data formats  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Dynamic filters and slicers for outlet, location, and product views  
- KPI cards for executive summary insights  
- Comparative visuals for cross-outlet performance  
- Drill-down capabilities from summary to transactional detail  

---

## 🧩 Conclusion

**RetailIQ** empowers e-commerce grocery businesses with a clear, data-driven view of sales performance and customer preferences. The dashboard supports informed decision-making for merchandising, regional targeting, and product development, enabling businesses to enhance both revenue and customer satisfaction.
